Dr. Kalpesh Sharadrao Bachhav — ENT & Head & Neck Surgeon

Dr. Kalpesh Bachhav is an ENT and head & neck surgeon based in Dhule. Consultations are unhurried, treatment is planned around each patient, and everything is explained in plain language.
